Privacy Policy

What LoopLeads collects, why we use it, and how you can request access or deletion.

Effective: 29 October 2026  ·  Contact: itskrish2654@gmail.com

LoopLeads ("we", "our", or "us") provides a web application that lets Instagram professional account owners configure automated replies to eligible comments and direct messages, share links, and collect customer enquiries. LoopLeads is operated from India. This policy covers information processed through the LoopLeads application and related support requests. Instagram and Meta have their own separate privacy practices.

Who this policy is for

It applies to (a) people who create and manage LoopLeads workspaces and connect Instagram accounts, and (b) Instagram users who interact with those connected accounts through features operated by LoopLeads.

1. Information we collect and receive

  • LoopLeads workspace details: account login email, a protected password hash, authentication/session information, and records of changes made in the workspace.
  • Connected Instagram accounts: Instagram account ID, username, available profile name and picture, connected-account status, published media identifiers and details needed to select posts or Reels, and the access tokens needed for authorized API operations.
  • Customer interactions: Instagram sender and comment/message IDs, usernames where supplied, eligible comment and DM content, media identifiers, timestamps, delivery results, and relevant conversation status.
  • Lead details: email addresses and phone numbers when a person chooses to provide them in a conversation, along with information about whether optional requests were skipped or configured requirements were met.
  • Automation settings: trigger keywords, selected media, message templates, button labels, destination links, and follow-check or lead-collection settings chosen by a workspace owner.
  • Security, service and support information: authentication attempts, system and webhook events, error information, and any messages you send to our support address.

We do not request your Instagram password. Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card information, government identifiers or other sensitive information in automated Instagram conversations.

2. How we use the information

  • Sign users into LoopLeads and restrict access to their own workspaces and connected Instagram accounts.
  • Connect Instagram professional accounts with authorization, show account identity and eligible posts/Reels, and manage account connection status.
  • Match eligible comments or DM keywords against an active automation and send responses permitted by Meta's messaging rules.
  • Display configurable buttons and links; request and validate email or phone details when the connected account owner enables lead collection.
  • Check an Instagram follow relationship where authorized and supported, before releasing a link configured as follow-protected.
  • Provide contact records, delivery status, exports and workspace activity; prevent duplicate events, investigate failures, maintain security and respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal duties and enforce our Terms of Service.

A connected business may configure an email or phone request as optional or required before releasing a link. Optional prompts can be skipped. Providing contact details during a conversation is separate from consenting to unrelated promotional messages. Connected businesses are responsible for appropriate notices and any permission needed before using leads for marketing.

3. Where information comes from

We receive account and conversation information from Meta's authorized Instagram APIs and webhooks, settings and login information directly from LoopLeads workspace users, and lead information from Instagram users who choose to reply to prompts.

4. Who can access information

Information related to an Instagram business account is made available to the authorized owner of that account through its LoopLeads workspace. We use Meta's Instagram APIs to receive events and send permitted responses. We use service providers for hosting and network operations, including AWS infrastructure and Cloudflare network services; our public legal pages are currently hosted using GitHub Pages. Support providers may process information needed to operate those services.

When a connected business includes a third-party website link, visiting that website is governed by that website's own privacy practices. We do not sell Instagram interaction records or use their contents for unrelated advertising targeting or training general-purpose AI models.

5. Security and storage

LoopLeads uses HTTPS, authenticated access controls and account-scoped data access. Passwords are stored as hashes; connected Instagram access tokens and captured lead email/phone fields are encrypted in the application database. Other comment/message content and identifiers are stored as operational records and are not represented as individually field-encrypted. Instagram webhooks are validated using signatures. These protections reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Application records are stored in PostgreSQL on cloud infrastructure. We do not represent that all processing or service-provider storage occurs in any particular country. Information may be processed in the locations where we and our infrastructure providers operate, subject to applicable rules.

6. How long information is kept

LoopLeads currently does not apply a single automatic deletion deadline to all lead, comment and message records. Records may remain while the connected account uses the service, until the workspace owner deletes a contact or disconnects the account, or until an appropriate verified deletion request is processed.

After an event is successfully processed, the application clears the raw webhook payload from its completed event record. A background maintenance process removes completed webhook-event records older than approximately 30 days. Failed or unresolved events and related contact/message records may be kept longer as needed for troubleshooting or until deletion.

Backup copies and limited security or operational records may persist for a period under backup rotation, applicable obligations, or incident-recovery requirements; deleting a visible contact does not necessarily remove every backup copy immediately. We do not currently promise a uniform backup deletion period.

7. Choices, access and deletion

  • Instagram users: request access, correction or deletion of information in LoopLeads by emailing itskrish2654@gmail.com. Include your Instagram username and the business account involved if known; never send an Instagram password.
  • Connected business owners: view and export account data from Data & exports, delete individual contacts from a contact's details page, and use Instagram Connections to disconnect an account and remove its operational records.
  • LoopLeads workspace users: request assistance with access, correction or deletion of their own LoopLeads login account by email. Full self-service workspace-user account deletion is not currently offered.

We may ask for limited information needed to verify a request, respond under applicable legal timelines, and explain if information must be kept for valid legal or security reasons. For the instructions, see our Data Deletion page.

Sending STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE or CANCEL in an Instagram DM is intended to stop further automation in the current workflow; it does not itself erase information already stored in LoopLeads.

8. Children

LoopLeads is intended for business and creator use and is not designed as a service for children. If you believe a child's information has been processed inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Updates to this policy

We may change this policy when product features, data handling or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top will identify the version published on this website.

10. Contact

For privacy, data requests or security concerns, email itskrish2654@gmail.com. Our Terms of Service explain account-owner responsibilities.
