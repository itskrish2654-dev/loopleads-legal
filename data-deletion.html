Data Deletion Instructions

How Instagram users and connected businesses can request access, correction or deletion of LoopLeads data.

Effective: 29 October 2026  ·  Contact: itskrish2654@gmail.com

Use this page to request deletion of personal information processed by LoopLeads. You do not need a LoopLeads login to send a data-deletion request.

Where to send your request

Email itskrish2654@gmail.com with the subject LoopLeads data deletion request. We will review and handle verified requests under applicable requirements.

1. If you commented on or messaged a business using LoopLeads

  1. Email us using the subject above.
  2. Include the Instagram username involved and, if known, the Instagram business account you contacted.
  3. Describe whether you want deletion, correction or a copy/explanation of the data associated with your interaction.
  4. Do not provide Instagram passwords, one-time codes, payment card numbers or government IDs. We may request limited verification so we do not delete someone else's records.

After verification, we will locate and remove eligible records we control, which may include the relevant contact, comment/message records, captured email/phone fields, and associated automation state. We may retain limited records where legally or operationally justified and will explain any material limitation. We respond within applicable legal timelines.

2. If you operate a LoopLeads workspace

  • Delete one contact: Sign in to LoopLeads, open Contacts, select the contact, and use the delete/erase option in the contact details page.
  • Export information: Open Data & exports and download the connected account's JSON export.
  • Disconnect a connected Instagram account: Open Instagram Connections and confirm the disconnect action. LoopLeads attempts to unsubscribe from Instagram webhooks and removes the connected account's operational data from the application database.
  • Delete your LoopLeads login account: Contact us by email. There is currently no complete self-service login-account deletion button.

Warning: Account disconnection removes its associated automation and contact data from LoopLeads. Export anything you legitimately need before confirming disconnection.

3. What deletion does and does not cover

Deletion covers applicable records stored and controlled by LoopLeads. It does not automatically remove comments or DMs from Instagram itself, information separately stored by the connected business outside LoopLeads, or data held by external websites that a customer visited. For those, contact the relevant service or business.

Some system and security records or backup copies may persist until they are rotated, are no longer needed for recovery, or are deleted as required by law. We cannot promise immediate removal from every historical backup. We do not impose a single automatic expiry on all lead records.

4. Stop messages versus delete data

A message such as STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE or CANCEL is intended to stop further automated replies within the current Instagram workflow. It is not a deletion request. If you also want stored records erased, use the email procedure above.

5. Privacy and contact

Read our Privacy Policy for details on collection, processing, security and retention. For help, email itskrish2654@gmail.com. Do not send private account credentials.
